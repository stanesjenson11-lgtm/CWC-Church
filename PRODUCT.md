# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Two groups. First-time visitors in Bengaluru (often young, often on a phone, usually arriving from the church's Instagram) who are deciding whether to show up on Sunday. And the existing congregation, who share the site and its verse with family and friends. Both arrive curious and leave with one thing: where and when to come, and a reason to come.

## Product Purpose
A single-page, scroll-driven story site for Compassion Worship Center (CWC), Hennur Gardens, Bengaluru. The story runs through a Bible opening to Joshua 24:15 ("as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD"), then Noah's ark ("come thou and all thy house into the ark"), and lands inside the church's own house: services in four languages, ministries, leaders, and directions. Success means a visitor who knows the time and the place and feels invited.

## Brand Personality
Bold, cinematic, reverent. A film title sequence that happens to be about a family church: huge type, hard cuts between black and white, and motion with weight. The words themselves stay humble and scriptural. The site should feel confident, never preachy, and never like a church-template.

## Anti-references
- Generic church templates: stock smiling-crowd photos, crosses/doves/clip-art icons, pastel gradients, "Welcome Home" hero over a blurred photo.
- The previous attempt: brown/walnut palette, decorative gradients, placeholder boxes. Called "sloppy" by the client.
- SaaS landing-page grammar: icon card grids, hero metrics, eyebrow labels on every section.
- References to borrow motion from, not visuals: fancycomponents.dev (text micro-interactions), skiper-ui.com, 21st.dev, gsap.com/scroll showcases, and the KIMI/GRID01 driver site (sticky receding stack, pixel-dissolve seams, contour backdrops, bracket panels, chamfered flood buttons, timeline rail).

## Design Principles
1. Let the verse carry the page. Every scene serves Joshua 24:15 or the invitation it leads to.
2. Motion with intent. Every scroll effect moves the story forward (open the book, enter the ark, come inside). None are decoration.
3. Real people, real place. Use the church's own photos and names. No stock images, no invented facts. Unknown details are left out rather than guessed.
4. Black and white with one red. The single accent is crimson (red-letter Bible, the heart in the logo). It is used sparingly, for scripture references and live/active states.
5. The invitation is never more than one scroll away: time, place, and directions are always reachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Every animation has a prefers-reduced-motion path (no smooth scroll, no pins, final states shown). Scripture text is real HTML, not images. The custom cursor is hidden on touch and never replaces focus states. Body text meets ≥4.5:1 contrast and large display text ≥3:1.
